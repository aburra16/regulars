# M3 My Circle Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A signed-in person taps Personalize, Brainstorm works out their circle, and the House picks / My circle toggle switches on. Scores in My circle are worked out at read time from the people the person trusts and the people they trust (brief §5 and §6, §13 step 5).

**Architecture:**
- **Tapping Personalize:**
  1. The app signs Brainstorm's login challenge (kind 22242).
  2. It trades the challenge for a token.
  3. It reads or starts a GrapeRank run.
  4. It polls the run until it is done.
- **Afterwards, the scores need no token.** The person's scorer, their "assistant" key, is found unauthenticated from `GET /setup/{pubkey}`. Its kind 30382 ranks are read from `wss://scores.brainstorm.world` with the M2a engine, exactly as the house's are.
- **Both points of view share the same store.** It holds both views' ranks, so toggling refetches nothing.

**Tech Stack:** as M2b. Plain `fetch` (no credentials) to `https://api.brainstorm.world`.

## Global Constraints

- **The Personalize tap is the only path to Brainstorm.**
  - Signing in to Regulars never contacts `api.brainstorm.world`. Only the Personalize tap does.
  - Brainstorm's sign-in creates the person's public scoring profile, and its scheduler then publishes their circle's ranks weekly. The line beside Personalize says so (decision 26, DRAFT): "Personalizing asks Brainstorm, our scoring partner, to work out your circle. It sets up a public scoring profile for you, and your circle's scores are public."
- **Login event:**
  - kind 22242, tags `[["t","brainstorm_login"],["challenge",<challenge>]]`, empty content;
  - signed by the account's signer;
  - one sign-in at a time, since the server keeps one challenge per pubkey.
- **Token:**
  - keep it in `sessionStorage` only;
  - send it as `Authorization: Bearer <jwt>`;
  - its life is 60 minutes; on a 401, ask the person to sign again only if they act (Update now), and never as a background prompt;
  - never put it in a URL, a log or `localStorage`.
- **Starting a run** (`POST /user/graperank`):
  - 429 "Too many requests" (per IP) or the quota 429, and 403 "too recent", all mean: "Your circle was updated recently. We'll use that." (brief §6). Then use the latest run.
  - A run is done when `internal_publication_status == "success"` and `ta_status == "success"`.
  - A run has failed when any of the three statuses is `"failure"`.
  - Poll every 15 s, with a cap of 45 minutes, and keep polling while the tab is open.
- **Weights for My circle:**
  - `rank / 100` at rank ≥ `config.scoring.line` (5, the same line as House picks);
  - an unranked reviewer is outside;
  - **the viewer's own review always counts in My circle, at full weight (1).**
- **No numbers on people** (decision 19).
  - "212 people in your circle" is a count of the circle, which is allowed.
  - No person's rank appears anywhere.
- **New network host:** `https://api.brainstorm.world`, only after the Personalize tap and only from then on.
- **Tests:**
  - wait for outcomes;
  - fake timers for polling;
  - no real network;
  - the copy gate;
  - the dark-mode guards.

## Review Focus

2. **Two tabs.** A person who taps Personalize in two tabs gets one sign-in, not a broken challenge. Test it in Task 1.

## Appendix: research notes (2026-10-08)

**The API:**
- `https://api.brainstorm.world` (from brainstorm.world/config.js), CORS `*`, no credentials;
- challenge `GET /authChallenge/{pk}`, which lives about 3 h and whose newest one wins;
- verify `POST /authChallenge/{pk}/verify {signed_event}`, giving `data.token`, a JWT of about 60 min.

**The Brainstorm sign-in:**
- It creates the assistant key.
- The prod scheduler then runs GrapeRank automatically, and weekly after that, publishing 30382s (rank ≥ 2) signed by the assistant to `wss://scores.brainstorm.world`.
- It also publishes the assistant's kind 0 ("<owner>'s Brainstorm Assistant") to damus, primal, nos.lol and nostr.band.
- The server never publishes the person's 10040.

**Runs:**
- `GET /user/graperankResult` gives `data: run | null`. `POST /user/graperank` takes no body.
- The run's statuses are `status`, `internal_publication_status` and `ta_status`, each one of waiting, ongoing, success or failure.
- Errors:
  - 429 per IP: 3 calls per 30 min, shared with followList;
  - 429 per account: 20 per 7 days;
  - 403 "too recent": within 30 min of any run.

**trustSignals** (token optional):
- at most 500 pubkeys, else 413;
- one bad pubkey gives 422 for the whole batch;
- `influence` is null until the person's first run is written;
- `verified` is influence > the preset cutoff (0.02 by default).

**`GET /setup/{pk}`:** unauthenticated; rows `["30382:<tag>", ta_pubkey, relay]`.
